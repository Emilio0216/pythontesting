<p>Stats and what they mean<p>
<p>max health = your max amount of health<p>
<p>health = current health<p>
<p>damage = how much base damage you'll do per successful attack<p>
<p>damage Bonus = the number added to a roll to see if the attack is successful and the number added to the damage<p>
<p>Crit chance = chance to crit /100<p>
<p>Defense = percent of damage reduced up to 90(%)<p>
<p>Max mana = max amount of mana<p>
<p>mana = amount of current mana<p>
<p>mana bonus = amount of mana recovered per turn<p>
<p>Speed = who starts first and % chance to dodge a successful attack up to 50% to dodge (speed isnt capped just the dodge chance is)<p>
<p>ability = character specific thing that character gets to do takes a turn to use<p>
<p>passive = side thing that's always active that's character specific<p>
